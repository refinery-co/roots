# Treat Week — Recharge

**Use:** Week 4 of every rotation — organic cycling
**Per:** 1 gallon
**NO MSU THIS WEEK**

---

## Mix

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Real Growers Recharge | 1/2 tsp (standalone — no MSU) |
| Hydroguard | 2ml (fresh — never bulk) |
| Microbe Life FGX | 1.5ml (fresh — never bulk) |
| Orca | 2ml (fresh — never bulk) |
| SNS 209 | 8ml |

**pH target:** 6.0

---

## What + Why

| | |
|---|---|
| What is Recharge | Mycorrhizae + Trichoderma + humic/fulvic + kelp |
| Why week 4 | Organic cycling fills gaps synthetic ferts can't cover |
| NEVER mix with | MSU or any synthetic fertilizer — defeats the biology |
| CALiMAGic | Not needed — no synthetic NPK this week |
| Duration | One watering only — restart cycle at week 1 standard |

'Cycling organics monthly keeps the biology varied' — Daryl Vendible IAS 2023

---

## Biologicals Rule

All biologicals fresh at pour time — maximum 1 hour in solution before applying. Recharge itself is shelf-stable dry but degrades once wet. Mix and apply immediately.
