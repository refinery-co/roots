# About Refinery Roots

Rodeo, California sits in the shadow of one of the country's largest oil refineries. Growing up there meant waking up to the smell of sulfur, running evacuation drills at school, watching the buffer zone between the plant and the neighborhood shrink and grow with the decades. That refinery is now a renewable energy facility. The buffer zone is still there.

Coming back to Rodeo means coming back to that history. What I found on the other side of it was gardening — tending anthuriums in a tent, learning soil biology, figuring out why a plant was struggling and what it actually needed. And separately, wine. Cocktails. The craft of making something from raw ingredients with enough patience and enough attention to get it right.

The name came naturally. A refinery takes something raw and processes it into something usable. That's what this is — raw material from every direction (textbooks, collector community knowledge, YouTube deep-dives, product labels, plant biology research) refined into protocols you can actually follow. Refinery Roots. Refinery Row for the drinks side.

Both are about the same thing: paying attention, tracing things back to their source, and doing the work properly instead of guessing.

---

## What This Is

A living knowledge base for growers who want to understand why, not just what.

Every protocol here traces back to a source — a label, a study, a collector with documented results. When sources conflict, the more conservative, plant-physiology-grounded position wins until evidence says otherwise. When something in here is wrong, it gets corrected and the reason is documented.

**For the casual reader:** Browse the protocols, follow the reference cards, share what's useful.

**For the grower who wants to run this system:** Download the repo, fork it, adapt it to your conditions and your collection. The protocols are starting points. Your environment and your plants will tell you where to adjust.

**For the researcher:** Every claim has a source. If you have better data, open a PR.
