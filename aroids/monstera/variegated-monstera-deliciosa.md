# Variegated Monstera deliciosa — Cultivar Guide

> Prices are USA market rates for 3+ leaf, A+ variegation specimens as of October 2024. Treat as a baseline, not a quote — the market moves.

## White (Albo) Variegation

### Albo variegata — Sectorial
- **Variegation:** Blocked patches, often half moons of white. Risk of reverting to all green or all white.
- **Origin:** Unknown
- **USA price (3+ leaf, A+ var):** $125–$225

### Albo variegata — Marbled
- **Variegation:** White, light green, dark green, marbled — referred to as "Kobe Beef" variegation.
- **Origin:** Unknown
- **USA price:** $125–$240

### Large Form Albo variegata
- **Variegation:** Same as standard Albo, but with stacked nodes (0.5–2cm internodal spacing), winged geniculums, inner perforations.
- **Large form identifier:** Stacked nodes are the definitive tell -- internodal spacing of 0.5–2cm between nodes. Easy to miss and commonly misidentified.
- **Origin:** Unknown
- **USA price:** $800–$1,800

## Yellow Variegation (Aurea)

### Aurea variegata — Sectorial
- **Variegation:** Blocked patches of yellow, often half moons. Starts green, burns yellow over time. Reversion risk.
- **Origin:** Likely seed-grown sport
- **USA price:** $150–$250

### Aurea variegata — Marbled
- **Variegation:** Yellow marble on dark green, striations of color. Starts lime green, becomes yellow.
- **Origin:** Unknown
- **USA price:** $125–$240

### Yellow Marilyn
- **Variegation:** Large form Aurea. Marbled yellow and neon lime-green against hunter green.
- **Origin:** Kunzo Nishihata, Japan
- **USA price:** $700–$1,200

### Super Orange
- **Variegation:** Similar to Yellow Marilyn (large form, marbled). Key difference: shade of yellow-orange.
- **Origin:** Brazil. Now grown in Indonesia by Mr. Noldy Topan.
- **USA price:** $1,500+

### Lemon Pompon
- **Variegation:** Cream, mint, yellow overlaying hunter green base. Likely large form.
- **Origin:** Unknown — likely Chinese or Thai
- **USA price:** $2,500+

## Thai Constellation (Cream / Flecked)

> Thai Constellation variegation is stable — from tissue culture. Unlike Albo, it does not revert.

### Standard Thai Constellation
- **Variegation:** Cream flecking resembling a constellation. Splashes of cream, mint green, white on hunter green.
- **Origin:** Found wild in Thailand in 1970s; propagated from tissue culture.
- **USA price:** $30–$80

### Creme Brûlée
- **Variegation:** Standard Thai Con with intense variegation — far less hunter green, heavily cream.
- **Origin:** Tissue culture
- **USA price:** $500–$1,000

### Green Snow
- **Variegation:** Similar to Creme Brûlée but with greener flecking instead of deep cream patches. Harder to differentiate from high-var Thai Cons.
- **Origin:** Tissue culture
- **USA price:** $500–$1,500

### Legacy
- **Variegation:** Super high-var Thai Con. Maximum flecking, less cream patch than Creme Brûlée, more than Platinum.
- **Origin:** Tissue culture
- **USA price:** $300–$800

### Platinum
- **Variegation:** Light white flecking, closer to mint pattern. No large patches of underlying variegation. Base green appears muted.
- **Origin:** Tissue culture
- **USA price:** $500–$1,500

### Medusa / Tattoo
- **Variegation:** Standard Thai Con with dark green stripes on undersides. Leaf morphology mutations — edges twist from aberrant cell structure.
- **Origin:** Tissue culture
- **USA price:** $4,000–$6,000

### Electrolyte / Electro Light
- **Variegation:** Similar to Creme Brûlée but lime green splashes instead of cream.
- **Origin:** Tissue culture
- **USA price:** $7,000–$9,000

### Tri-Color / Miracle
- **Variegation:** Contains all three variegations — Thai Con flecking, Aurea yellow-green, Albo patches.
- **Origin:** Lopuri, Thailand tissue culture lab
- **USA price:** $8,000–$12,000+

### Siam Mint
- **Variegation:** Hunter green base with yellow variegation in mint-like pattern throughout.
- **Lineage:** Likely Mint lineage -- not Thai Con as previously suggested. Visual similarity to white monster but with yellow/green variegation instead of white. (Chris, Pretty in Green video Oct 2024: "I'm starting to think it comes from the Mint and not the Thai Con.")
- **Origin:** Unknown
- **USA price:** $3,000–$6,000

### Starlight
- **Variegation:** Flecked meteor-like variegation in minty green and white, with Thai Con underneath.
- **Origin:** Tissue culture
- **USA price:** $10,000+

## Mint-Like Sports

### Mint
- **Variegation:** White and minty green striations throughout. Full mint = no hunter green blocks visible.
- **Origin:** Seed sport from Japan, WWII era
- **USA price:** $500–$900

### White Monster
- **Variegation:** White dominant, slight green venation. Leaves begin highly white, veins darken over time.
- **Origin:** Kunzo Nishihata, Japan
- **USA price:** $500–$900+

### Jungle Mint (NOID)
- **Variegation:** Deep greens, minty greens, sectorial, with Albo and mint patches. Wide splits and perforations.
- **Origin:** Barry Schwartz
- **USA price:** $800–$1,400

### Thrasher Sport Mint
- **Variegation:** Sectorial multi-type — mint, Albo, green-on-green, some near-Aurea.
- **Origin:** USA — Tyler Thrasher seed sport
- **USA price:** $1,500–$3,000

### Bulbasaur
- **Variegation:** Mint green dominant, minor hunter green showing. Multiple shades of mint.
- **Origin:** Seed sport found by Yang Liu
- **USA price:** $12,000–$18,000

### Devil Monster / Circuit Monstera
- **Variegation:** White Monster coloring with ribcage-like leaf morphology. Currently the rarest variegated Monstera on the market.
- **Origin:** Likely China — first seen on Instagram September 2023
- **USA price:** $25,000–$40,000

### Viet Truong Sa
- **Variegation:** Mint green dominant, minor hunter green. Similar to Bulbasaur and Shamrock Shake.
- **Origin:** Ethyl methanesulfonate-induced whole-plant mutation of M. deliciosa
- **USA price:** $4,000–$12,000

### Shamrock Shake
- **Variegation:** Mint green dominant, hunter green venation. New leaves lime green, mute as they age. Flecked and sectorial.
- **Origin:** Florida seed tray sport
- **USA price:** $4,000–$6,000

## Contorted Sports

### Masterpiece
- **Variegation:** Multi-layered yellows, mint greens, hunter green, flecked minty greens — described as "layered like an oil painting." Morphology mutations typical.
- **Origin:** Florida seed grown by Michael D'Andrea
- **USA price:** $5,000–$6,000

### Silver Sands
- **Variegation:** Flecked silver and white variegation on hunter green. Contorted, round leaves.
- **Origin:** Purchased at First Annual International Exotic Plant Show, Chiang Mai, October 2022. Sport by Michael D'Andrea.
- **USA price:** $3,800–$8,000

### Ocean Mint
- **Variegation:** Large form mint-like with Oceana-like markings, broader range of mint colors.
- **Origin:** Seed grown sport, possibly Mike D'Andrea
- **USA price:** $1,500–$2,500

### Oceana
- **Variegation:** Markings similar to ocean waves or foam. Registered cultivar.
- **Origin:** Seed grown sport by Mike D'Andrea, sold and registered by Logan Conley
- **USA price:** $800–$1,500

### White Lava
- **Variegation:** Ocean wave-like markings (similar to Oceana).
- **Origin:** Seed grown sport from Thailand
- **USA price:** $2,000–$3,000

## Green Variegated

### Green on Green (GoG) — Sectorial
- **Variegation:** Blocked patches of lime green, often half moons. Reversion risk to all green or lime yellow.
- **Origin:** Seed sport
- **USA price:** $200–$400

### Green on Green (GoG) — Marbled
- **Variegation:** Light lime green against dark green marbled striations — can be hard to see until 2–3 weeks after unfurling.
- **Origin:** Unknown
- **USA price:** $200–$500
