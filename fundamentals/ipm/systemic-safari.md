# Systemic Drench — Safari 20SG

**Root drench — max once per 3 months — per 1 gallon**

---

## Mix

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Safari 20SG (Dinotefuran) | 1 tsp |

**pH target:** 6.0–6.5

---

## How It Works

| | |
|---|---|
| Active ingredient | Dinotefuran 20% — neonicotinoid class |
| Mode of action | Systemic via roots — plant becomes toxic to pests |
| Speed | 1–3 days to effectiveness in containers |
| Best against | Mealybugs, scale, whitefly, aphids, thrips |
| Frequency | Once per 3 months — label maximum, do not exceed |
| Resistance | Rotate active ingredients per pesticide specialist |

---

## When to Use

Reserve for serious or established infestations — not preventative. SNS 209 (rosemary oil) runs at every watering as the preventative layer. Safari is the escalation when that fails.

'If you've got a $4,000 plant — systemic all the way.' — Daryl Vendible (Tezula / IAS 2023)
