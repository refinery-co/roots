# Quarantine Protocol

**Every new arrival — no exceptions — 3–4 week hold minimum**

---

## Day by Day

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | IPA wipe — all leaf surfaces, top and bottom |
| Day 1 | Spinosad foliar — all surfaces, undersides first |
| Day 1 | Inspect roots — trim rot, dip in Hydroguard solution |
| Days 1–14 | No fertilizer — use acclimation mix only |
| Day 7 | Azamax foliar — catches eggs hatched since day 1 |
| Day 14 | Spinosad — final knockdown before collection entry |
| Day 14+ | Begin standard nutrition only if plant looks healthy |
| Weeks 3–4 | Hold in isolation — watch new growth for pests |

---

## Environment + Clearance

| | |
|---|---|
| Location | Tent or isolated space — not near main collection |
| Monitor | New growth for distortion, silvering, stippling, webbing |
| Clear to add | No visible pests on two consecutive inspections |

---

## Why It Matters

A single infected plant can wipe out a collection in weeks.

The Day 1 + Day 7 + Day 14 spray sequence is deliberate:
- Day 1 spinosad kills active adults and nymphs
- Day 7 Azamax catches eggs that hatched after Day 1 (thrips eggs hatch in 5–7 days)
- Day 14 spinosad is the final knockdown before the plant enters the main collection

Skipping any step leaves a generation gap that survives into your collection.
