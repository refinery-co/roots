# Inoculate at Watering — Biologicals

**Add fresh every session — never pre-mix — per gallon drawn**

---

## Add to Each Can — Never Pre-Mix

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Hydroguard | 2ml |
| Microbe Life FGX | 1.5ml |
| Orca | 2ml |

**Workflow:** Fill can from reservoir → add biologicals → swirl → water immediately.
Never let a dosed can sit — redose fresh for each can drawn.

---

## Why Fresh Only

| | |
|---|---|
| Why fresh only | Live organisms — die in solution within 24–48 hours |
| Hydroguard | *Bacillus* consumes O₂ in reservoir — dead by next day |
| Microbe Life FGX | Live microbes — same stability as Hydroguard |
| Orca | Mycorrhizal spores degrade with pH + EC drift over time |
| Recharge rule | Mix fresh, water all plants within 1 hour — no exceptions |
| At repotting | Bump Orca to 5ml/gal — pour direct on exposed roots |
| Reservoir safe | MSU, silica, kelp, Rapidstart, SNS 209 — stable 5–7 days |

---

## What Can Live in the Reservoir

Safe to pre-mix and store 5–7 days:
- MSU 13-3-15 or 19-4-23
- TPS Silica Gold (after 30-min wait before adding other ingredients)
- Bloom City Kelp
- GH Rapidstart
- SNS 209

Add biologicals (Hydroguard, FGX, Orca) only at the moment of watering.
