# Foliar N — Nutrient Spray

**Frequency:** Every session — standalone on Spinosad weeks, built into Azamax weeks
**Per:** 1 gallon

---

## Mix Order — Add in This Sequence

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| TPS Silica Gold | 8ml |
| **— WAIT 30 MINUTES —** | |
| Bloom City Clean Kelp | 8ml |
| Foliage Pro 9-3-6 | 1ml |

**pH target:** 6.0–6.5

---

## What Each Does

| Ingredient | Role |
|---|---|
| TPS Silica Gold | Strengthens cell walls — physical barrier against pests and environmental stress |
| Bloom City Clean Kelp | Cytokinins, auxins, trace minerals — growth regulation, stress response |
| Foliage Pro 9-3-6 | Balanced NPK foliar feed — 1ml/gal for leaf application (not drench rate) |

---

## When It Runs

| Week | How it runs |
|---|---|
| Azamax weeks (A) | Nutrients are **built into Spray A** — no separate N session needed |
| Spinosad weeks (B) | Run **N on a different night** from B — silica residue can interfere with spinosad |
| Copper weeks (C) | **Skip N that session** — copper runs fully standalone |

---

## Shelf Life

| Product | Unopened | Once Opened |
|---|---|---|
| TPS Silica Gold | 2+ years | 1–2 years — keep sealed, away from light |
| Bloom City Clean Kelp | 1–2 years | 6–12 months — refrigerate once opened, cytokinins degrade |
| Foliage Pro 9-3-6 | 2+ years | 1+ year — keep sealed |

**Mixed spray:** no shelf life — mix fresh every session. Kelp cytokinins break down within 24 hours in solution. pH drifts once silica is added. No batch-ahead.

---

## Notes

- Year-round, no seasonal adjustment needed under artificial light
- Evenings only — spray all surfaces including undersides — low light
- Foliage Pro foliar rate is 1ml/gal — the drench rate is higher; do not scale up for foliar
- Sources: TPS Silica Gold label (8ml/gal foliar), Bloom City Kelp label (5–10ml/gal), Foliage Pro label (foliar 1ml/gal)
