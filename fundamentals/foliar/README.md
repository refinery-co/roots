# Foliar Spray Protocols

Four sprays total: one nutrient base, three pest/disease rotations. The nutrient spray runs every session. Pest sprays rotate on a two-week cycle. Copper runs independently.

---

## The Schedule

| Week | Evening Session 1 | Evening Session 2 (if needed) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | **N — Nutrients** (silica + kelp + Foliage Pro) | **B — Spinosad** (standalone, same week different night) |
| Week 2 | **A — Azamax** (nutrients built in) | — |
| Week 3 | **N — Nutrients** (silica + kelp + Foliage Pro) | **B — Spinosad** (standalone, same week different night) |
| Week 4 | **A — Azamax** (nutrients built in) | — |
| Month (independent) | **C — Copper** (standalone — replaces N that session) | — |

**Spinosad weeks:** run N and B on different nights — never the same session. Silica from the N spray will still be in the air/residue and can interfere.

---

## The Four Sprays

| Spray | Active | Stackable? | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **N — Nutrients** | Silica + Kelp + Foliage Pro | Yes — base of Spray A | Weekly (standalone on B weeks) |
| **A — Azamax** | Azadirachtin (IGR) | Yes — includes N stack | Every 2 weeks |
| **B — Spinosad** | Spinosyn A+D | **Standalone only** | Every 2 weeks |
| **C — Copper** | Copper octanoate | **Standalone only** | Every 3–4 weeks |

---

## Critical Rules

1. **Silica first, always** — 8ml TPS Silica Gold, wait 30 minutes before adding anything else
2. **Spinosad standalone** — silica raises pH and degrades spinosad; B runs in clean pH-adjusted water only, different night from N
3. **Copper standalone** — kills beneficials and biologicals; 48-hour wait after copper before any biological drench or release
4. **Evenings only** — all sessions; low light; all surfaces including undersides
5. **Beneficials gap** — wait 1–2 weeks after spinosad before releasing Stratiolaelaps or Swirskii
6. **Foliage Pro at 1ml/gal foliar** — higher concentration is for drench; stay at 1ml for leaf application
