# Foliar Spray Protocols

Three-spray rotation for indoor aroids. Each spray targets different pest/disease vectors. Alternate A and B to prevent resistance buildup. C runs on its own schedule.

## The Rotation

| Spray | Active | Frequency | Stack? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Azamax** | Azadirachtin (IGR) | Every 2 weeks | Silica + kelp + Foliage Pro |
| **B — Spinosad** | Spinosyn A+D | Every 2 weeks | **Standalone only** |
| **C — Copper** | Copper octanoate | Every 3–4 weeks | **Standalone only** |

A and B alternate each week: Week 1 → A, Week 2 → B, Week 3 → A, Week 4 → B.
C runs independently on its own 3–4 week calendar — not tied to the A/B cycle.

## Critical Rules

1. **Silica first, always** — 30-minute wait before anything else (Sprays A only)
2. **Spinosad standalone** — silica raises pH and degrades spinosad; run B in clean water only
3. **Copper standalone** — kills beneficials and biologicals; 48-hour wait after copper before any biological application
4. **Evenings only** — all foliar sprays; low light; spray all surfaces including undersides
5. **Beneficials gap** — wait 1–2 weeks after spinosad before releasing Stratiolaelaps or Swirskii
