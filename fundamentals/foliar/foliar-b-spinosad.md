# Foliar Spray B — Spinosad

**Frequency:** Every 2 weeks — alternate with Foliar A (Azamax)
**Per:** 1 gallon
**⚠ STANDALONE ONLY — do not combine with silica, kelp, or nutrients**

---

## Mix

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Captain Jack's Deadbug Brew (or Monterey Garden Insect Spray) | 4ml (1 tsp) |

**pH target:** 6.0–6.5 (adjust with pH down before adding spinosad)

---

## Why Standalone

Spinosad degrades rapidly in alkaline conditions. Silica raises solution pH significantly — mixing spinosad after silica (even after the 30-minute wait) accelerates breakdown and cuts efficacy before it reaches the leaf. Run spinosad alone in clean, pH-adjusted water only.

Foliage Pro, kelp, and silica go in the **Foliar A** run. This card is spinosad only.

---

## How It Works

| | |
|---|---|
| Mode of action | Affects insect nervous system — contact + ingestion |
| Best against | Thrips adults, spider mites, caterpillars, leafminers |
| Systemic? | No — contact only, must hit the pest directly |
| Reapply cycle | Every 7–10 days, minimum 2–3 cycles |
| OMRI listed | Yes (both brands) |

**Captain Jack's vs. Monterey:** Captain Jack's contains spinosyn A + D (more effective against thrips and spider mites). Prefer Captain Jack's when available.

---

## Beneficials Warning

Spinosad is toxic to Stratiolaelaps, Swirskii, and all beneficial insects **while wet**.
- Wait **1–2 weeks** after spinosad application before introducing or releasing beneficials
- Do not apply spinosad to soil or media where Stratiolaelaps are established

---

## Notes

- Evenings only — spray all surfaces including undersides — low light only
- Shake well before use (both brands)
- Next spray session switch to Foliar A (Azamax) to prevent resistance
- Source: Captain Jack's label — 1 tsp per gallon for most pests
