# Nutrient Protocols

Four-week rotation for indoor aroids under artificial light. Designed around MSU orchid fertilizers and biological soil amendments.

## The Rotation

| Week | Formula | Key ingredient | CALiMAGic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MSU 13-3-15 Base | 1 tsp/gal | No — Mg included |
| 2 | MSU 13-3-15 Base | 1 tsp/gal | No — Mg included |
| 3 | MSU 19-4-23 Push | 1/2 tsp/gal | MANDATORY 2ml |
| 4 | Recharge Treat Week | 1/2 tsp/gal | No MSU this week |

Repeat from week 1. Year-round — no seasonal adjustment needed under artificial light.

## Critical Rules

1. **Silica first, always** — TPS Silica Gold 3ml, wait 30 minutes before adding anything else
2. **CALiMAGic mandatory on push week** — 19-4-23 has zero magnesium; deficiency without it
3. **Never double-push** — week 4 is always Recharge, never two 19-4-23 weeks in a row
4. **Biologicals fresh at pour time** — Hydroguard, FGX, Orca die in solution within 24–48 hours; mix immediately before watering, never in advance
5. **Orca off on push week** — high EC interferes with mycorrhizal colonization
6. **Recharge standalone** — never mix with MSU or any synthetic fertilizer

## Pine Bark Note

Orchiata and other pine bark media consume 10–15% of available nitrogen through microbial decomposition. MSU 13-3-15 compensates for this — do not adjust down on bark-heavy mixes.
